Daily puzzle

Five letters, six guesses, one pregnancy-themed word a day. Solving it reveals a small note worth knowing, and the result is shareable.

Six guesses. Green is right, gold is the wrong spot.

Why a word game

Research behind this project found that digital pregnancy tracking falls off sharply over nine months, with only about a quarter of participants completing assessments consistently. A daily habit worth returning to is what keeps the wellness record complete enough to be useful at an appointment. Play the puzzle, then take a few seconds for the check-in.
