Postpartum Depression

Depression can start during pregnancy or in the year after birth, and it's common and treatable. This mood check is ten questions about how you've felt in the past 7 days. It takes about two minutes.

How have you been feeling lately?

Pregnancy and the months after birth can bring big changes in mood. This check helps you notice how you've been doing, so you can bring it up with your provider. It doesn't diagnose anything.
