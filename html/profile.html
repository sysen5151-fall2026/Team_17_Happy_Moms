Your data

Happy Moms has no accounts and no server. Everything you enter is stored by this browser on this device, which means clearing site data or switching devices loses it unless you export first.

Sharing with a support person

There is no automatic sharing in this release, by design: you send what you choose. Puzzle results copy to your clipboard, and visit notes copy, download, or print. A partner view with its own permissions is planned, and would need accounts and a server, which this deployment deliberately does without.
