Common questions

Before you write

Where is my data?

In your browser on this device, and nowhere else. We cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you. Export a copy from profile and data.

My history disappeared

Clearing site data, private browsing, or a different browser or device all start you fresh, since there is no account holding it together. A previous export can be restored from the same page.

Can I use this instead of my doctor?

No, and it is built not to be. Happy Moms tracks and summarizes what you report. Diagnosis, prescriptions, and interpretation stay with your provider.
