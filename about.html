About Happy Moms

Gentle guidance, built around real life

We started Happy Moms because pregnancy advice online is often overwhelming, contradictory, or wrapped in fear. We wanted something calmer.

Our Mission

Helping every mom feel informed, not anxious

Happy Moms exists to make healthy pregnancy habits feel approachable. Instead of long lists of rules, we focus on clear, bite-sized guidance across nutrition, movement, rest, and emotional wellbeing — plus a short, reflective check-in that helps you see where you're already doing well and where a little extra care could help.

We believe informed moms make confident decisions, and confident moms feel calmer through pregnancy. Everything here is meant to sit alongside — never replace — the care of your doctor, midwife, or OB-GYN.

What We Value

The principles behind everything we share

Evidence-informed

Our tips reflect widely-shared guidance from prenatal health resources, kept simple and practical — always meant to complement, not replace, your provider's advice.

Compassionate, not preachy

No fear-based messaging, no perfect-mom pressure. Just warm, realistic suggestions that meet you where you are.

Made for real questions

Our Wellness Check-In was built around the questions moms actually ask themselves — quietly, at 2am, or in the car after an appointment.

Team 17

Built by people who care about maternal wellbeing

Happy Moms is a project by Team 17, brought together to make trustworthy pregnancy wellness guidance easier to find and easier to act on.

A gentle reminder: Happy Moms provides general wellness information for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and does not replace the guidance of a licensed healthcare provider. Always talk with your doctor or midwife about what's right for you and your pregnancy.
