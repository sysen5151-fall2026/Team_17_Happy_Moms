Why we built it

Two things go wrong between prenatal visits

Both are well documented, and both are fixable with a lighter tool rather than a bigger one.

Tracking quietly stops

In a study of 585 pregnant women using a digital health platform, only about a quarter completed the requested assessments consistently, and most missed at least one entirely. Logging that feels like paperwork does not survive nine months.

Answers come from anywhere

Roughly 73% of pregnant women surveyed in the United States looked for health information online, where accuracy is uneven and unconstrained chatbots will confidently invent things. Everyday questions deserve a safer default.

Brusniak et al., JMIR mHealth and uHealth, 2020. Hsiao et al., Women & Health, 2023. Full citations on the about page.

How it works

Three steps, then it mostly runs itself

1. Set your baseline

Add your due date and answer six questions about where you are starting. That becomes the reference point everything later is read against.

2. Check in daily

Mood, energy, sleep, nausea, water, movement, and anything else going on. Single taps, seven in all. The daily puzzle is the nudge that brings you back.

3. Bring the notes

Before an appointment, generate a one-page summary of the interval: averages, what changed, recurring symptoms, and your saved questions. Copy, print, or keep it.

Where the line is

A wellness tool, deliberately not a clinical one

Drawing that boundary early was the point of our systems analysis. Happy Moms helps you notice and remember. Interpreting what it means stays with the people qualified to do it.

  • Tracks what you report and summarizes it back to you
  • Answers everyday questions from vetted, cited content
  • Routes warning signs straight to your provider or a crisis line

It does not diagnose, prescribe, interpret symptoms, connect to any health record, or alert anyone on your behalf.

Your data

Nothing leaves your device unless you send it

There are no accounts and no server. Your check-ins live in your own browser, which keeps sensitive health notes out of anyone else's hands, including ours.

  • Export everything as a file whenever you want a backup
  • Delete all of it in one click, with nothing left behind
  • Share a summary only by copying, printing, or downloading it yourself
