Wellness assistant

Everyday pregnancy questions, answered from a fixed library the team wrote in advance. Nothing is generated on the fly, so the answers cannot drift off source.

How this is kept safe

What it does

  • Answers from a library of vetted wellness topics, written in advance
  • Names the organization behind each answer, with a link
  • Screens every question for warning signs before answering anything
  • Saves anything it will not answer as a question for your provider

What it will not do

  • Diagnose, interpret your symptoms, or read your check-in data
  • Tell you whether a medication or supplement is safe for you
  • Generate new text with a language model, in this release
  • Replace a call to your provider about anything urgent

Content is paraphrased general guidance from ACOG, NIH, FDA, and CDC, pending clinical review. A future release may use a language model to rephrase these same approved sources, behind the same screening.
